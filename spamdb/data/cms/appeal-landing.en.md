# Your account has been closed

Your account was closed because of a breach of the
[Terms of Service](/terms-of-service). In most cases that means engine assistance during
games, but it can also follow repeated abuse, evading a previous restriction, or
running multiple accounts.

## If you think this is a mistake

Mistakes do happen, and we would rather correct one than defend it. Send an
appeal through the appeal page. Explain your side plainly — what you were doing,
what you use to play, whether anyone else has access to your account.

Please do not:

- message moderators directly, or open a second account to ask about the first
- send the same appeal repeatedly; it is read once and does not move faster

## If you did what you were marked for

Say so. An account closed for cheating stays closed, but an honest admission is
the difference between being allowed to start again later and not being allowed.
Denying something we have the game record for does not help you.

## What stays

Your games remain visible, because they involve your opponents too. Your rating
history stops where the account did.
