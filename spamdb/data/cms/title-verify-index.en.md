# Title Verification

If you hold a chess title awarded over the board, you can have it shown next to
your username here.

## Titles we show

Grandmaster (GM), International Master (IM), FIDE Master (FM), Candidate Master
(CM), and the women's equivalents (WGM, WIM, WFM, WCM). We also show National
Master where the national federation awards it.

## How to apply

Use the [verification form](/verify-title). You will be asked for:

1. **Your full name** as it appears in the FIDE or federation records.
2. **Your FIDE profile link**, or the federation page listing your title.
3. **Proof that the account is yours** — usually a photo of yourself holding a
   note with your username and today's date, or a link from a profile you
   control that points back at this account.

The second and third points are separate on purpose. The first proves the title
exists; the third proves it is yours.

## What happens next

We check the application by hand, so it is not instant. If something is missing
we will ask through the site's messaging rather than by email.

Once verified, the title appears next to your username everywhere on the site,
and you become eligible for titled-player events.

## Please note

Titles earned only online are not shown. A false claim will cost you the account,
not just the title.
