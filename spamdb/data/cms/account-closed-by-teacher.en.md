# Your account has been closed by your teacher

This account was created for you through a class, and your teacher has closed
it. Teachers can do this for accounts they manage.

## What this means

You cannot sign in to this account any more. Your games and puzzle history stay
where they are, but you cannot play, post, or change anything.

## If you want it back

Ask your teacher. They can reopen an account they closed — we cannot do it for
them, because the account belongs to their class rather than to us.

## If you have left the class

You are welcome to [create your own account](/signup). It will be yours, not
managed by anyone, and you can keep it after school. It starts fresh: ratings
and history do not transfer from a class account.

If you are under the age your country sets for consenting to data storage, use
the site with a parent's knowledge, and consider turning on Kid Mode.
