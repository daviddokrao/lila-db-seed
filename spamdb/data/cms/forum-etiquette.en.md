# Forum Etiquette

The forum works when threads are worth opening. A few habits keep it that way.

## Before you post

**Look for an existing thread.** Most questions have been asked. A reply to a
live thread gets more answers than a new thread saying the same thing.

**Pick the right board.** Off-topic conversation belongs in the off-topic board,
not in the middle of an analysis thread.

**Write a title that says something.** "Question" and "Help!!" tell nobody
whether they can help you. "Why is this rook endgame drawn?" gets answered.

## In the thread

**Show the position.** Paste a game link or a FEN — the forum renders both as a
board. A described position is a position nobody will check.

**Do not bump.** Posting "any thoughts?" pushes your thread up and everyone
else's down. If it got no answers, it may need a better question rather than more
visibility.

**Disagree with the argument.** "That plan loses a tempo because…" is a
contribution. "That is stupid" is not.

**Do not accuse people of cheating.** Not by name, not by hint. Use the
[report form](/report); it is the only route that does anything.

## Things that get posts removed

Everything in the [Communication Guidelines](/page/communication-guidelines)
applies here. On top of that: advertising, reposting the same thing across
boards, and threads about moderation decisions — those belong in an
[appeal](/appeal), where the people who can act on them will read it.

## Moderation

Moderators can delete posts, close threads and remove forum access. If your post
disappeared and you cannot see why, ask through [contact](/contact) rather than
opening a thread about it.
