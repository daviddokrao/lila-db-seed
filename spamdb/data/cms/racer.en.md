# Puzzle Racer

Puzzle Storm, but against other people at the same time.

## How it works

Everyone in the race gets **the same puzzles in the same order**, and everyone
gets the same time. Whoever solves the most wins. A mistake does not end your
run, but it costs you time you cannot get back.

You can race strangers by joining a public race, or send a link to friends and
race only them. Racing friends is the more interesting version — you can compare
where each of you slowed down afterwards.

## Getting better at it

**Do not watch the leaderboard mid-race.** The seconds you spend looking at
somebody else's score are seconds you are not solving.

**Accuracy beats speed here more than in Storm.** Everyone has identical
puzzles, so a race is usually decided by who made fewer mistakes rather than by
who clicked faster.

**Review the race afterwards.** Since everybody solved the same set, the puzzle
where you lost the lead is a precise, personal diagnosis.
