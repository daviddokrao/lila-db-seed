# Chess Calendar

Everything scheduled, in one place: broadcasts of real tournaments, and the
arenas and swisses running here on the site.

## Reading it

Times are shown in **your own timezone**, taken from your browser, so you never
have to convert anything.

Each entry links straight to the event. Before it starts, that page tells you
the format and lets you look at the players; once it starts, the same link is
where you watch or play.

## Following something

Opening an event page before it begins is enough to find your way back — it will
appear in your recent pages. For tournaments you intend to play, join in advance
rather than at the start time; arenas let you enter late but you lose the
minutes you were not there.

## Nothing listed?

Then nothing is scheduled at the moment. HungKings is new, so quiet stretches
are normal. [Broadcasts](/broadcast) shows anything running right now, and the
[tournaments](/tournament) page always has arenas open to join.
