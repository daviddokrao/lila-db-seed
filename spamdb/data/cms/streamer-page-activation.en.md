# Getting Your Streamer Page Live

A streamer page puts you in the [streamer list](/streamer) and marks your profile
as live while you are broadcasting chess.

## Setting it up

1. Open your streamer page from your account settings and fill it in: a headline,
   a short description, and a picture.
2. Add **your channel**, on Twitch or YouTube. This is the part people forget —
   without it there is nothing for us to check.
3. Submit it for approval.

## Approval

A human reviews it. What we are checking is simply that the channel is yours and
that it streams chess. That is all.

Once approved, your page stays approved. You do not reapply.

## Going live

While your channel is streaming chess, you appear at the top of the streamer list
and your profile shows as live. The site checks your channel periodically, so
there is a delay of a few minutes at the start and end of a stream — this is
normal and not something to report.

If you stream something other than chess, you simply will not be listed during
that stream. Nothing is penalised.

## Common reasons a page is not approved

- **No channel link**, or a link to a channel that does not exist
- **A channel with no chess content** yet — stream first, apply after
- **A channel that is not yours.** Linking someone else's channel is
  impersonation and costs the account

## While streaming

Fair play rules do not change because you are on camera, and there are a few
specific traps — see [Fair Play While Streaming](/page/streaming-fairplay-faq).
