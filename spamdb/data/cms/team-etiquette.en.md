# Team Etiquette

Teams work well when they feel like a club rather than a mailing list. A few
conventions make that easier.

## For team leaders

**Say what the team is for** in the description. "Vietnamese players", "under
1500 only", "we run a weekly blitz arena on Sunday" — anything that lets someone
decide whether to join before they do.

**Use the forum, not mass messages.** A team message goes to every member's
inbox. Reserve it for things that are genuinely time-sensitive, like a
tournament starting in an hour. Everything else belongs in the team forum, where
people read it when they want to.

**Do not recruit by spamming.** Sending join requests or advertisements to
players who never asked is the fastest way to have a team shut down.

**Answer join requests.** Leaving people waiting for weeks is worse than
declining them.

## For members

**Read the description before joining**, especially rating limits.

**Turn up for team battles you signed up for.** A team battle where half the
roster does not appear is a loss for everyone who did.

**Keep the team forum on topic.** General chess chat has the
[main forum](/forum).

## Rules that are not optional

Everything in the [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service) applies inside teams too. A private
team forum is not a place where harassment or hate speech becomes acceptable.
Team leaders are responsible for what stays up in their own forum.
