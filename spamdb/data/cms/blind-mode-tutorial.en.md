# Accessibility and Blind Mode

HungKings can be played without seeing the board. Blind mode replaces the visual
board with a text interface that works with a screen reader.

## Turning it on

The link is at the very top of every page — it is the first thing a screen reader
reaches, before the navigation. Activate **Accessibility: enable blind mode** and
the setting is remembered for your account.

## How you play

The board is presented as text you can read line by line, and you enter moves by
typing them in algebraic notation: `e4`, `Nf3`, `exd5`, `O-O`. Press Enter to
play the move.

You can ask for information at any time without using a move:

- the position, read out square by square or piece by piece
- the last move played
- both clocks
- the material on each side

## Keyboard control

Everything is reachable from the keyboard, not only the board. Press **?** on most
pages for the shortcuts available there. Analysis, puzzles and studies all accept
moves typed the same way.

## What works well and what does not

Playing, puzzles, and analysis are all usable. Correspondence and slower time
controls are more comfortable than bullet, for the obvious reason.

Some pages are still better than others. If you hit one that is not usable, that
is a bug worth reporting — [tell us](/contact) which page and what your screen
reader does there. Accessibility problems are hard for sighted developers to
notice on their own, so a specific report is genuinely useful.

## For everyone else

Blind mode is also a fast keyboard-only way to play, and some sighted players use
it for that. It is not restricted.
