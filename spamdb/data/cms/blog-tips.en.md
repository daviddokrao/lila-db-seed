# Blog Tips

Anyone with an account can write a blog here. A few things make a post worth
finishing.

## Start with the position

A chess post is read next to a board. Paste a game link or a FEN and it renders
as a playable board inline — readers can move the pieces while they read. A post
that describes a position in prose asks the reader to do work you could have done
for them.

## One idea per post

"Three endgames I misplayed last month" is a post. "My chess journey" is a
diary. The first has something a stranger can take away.

## Write about your own games

Your losses are more interesting than grandmaster games, because you can say what
you were actually thinking when you went wrong. Nobody else can write that post.

## Practical things

- **The cover image** is what people see in the list. Posts without one are
  scrolled past
- **The intro** is shown on the card. Write it as a hook, not as the first
  paragraph cut short
- **Pick topics honestly** — they are how people find posts on subjects they care
  about
- **Set it public** when it is ready. A post left private never appears anywhere

## Getting read

New blogs on a new site have few readers; that is arithmetic, not a judgement of
the writing. Posts can be featured on the home page carousel, and posting a link
in a relevant forum thread is fair — spraying it across unrelated boards is not.

The rules are in [Blog Etiquette](/page/blog-etiquette).
