You must be old enough to consent to us storing your data where you live. If you

## Fair play

Do not use a chess engine, a database, another person, or any other outside help
during a game, unless the game explicitly allows it. This is the one rule we
enforce hardest, because it is the only one that ruins the site for everyone
else.

Do not deliberately lose games, arrange results, or play in a way designed to
manipulate ratings — yours or anyone else's.

Do not stall a lost position to run down the clock, and do not abandon games you
are losing. Repeatedly leaving games will suspend your ability to start new ones
for a while.

was wrong, appeal — do not message moderators directly.
