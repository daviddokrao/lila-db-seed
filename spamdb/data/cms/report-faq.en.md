# Reporting a Player

## When to report

Report someone when they cheated, abused another player, or behaved in a way the
[Terms of Service](/terms-of-service) forbid. Use the [report form](/report), or the report
button on their profile page.

## What makes a report useful

**Name the games.** A report saying "he cheated" gives moderators nothing to look
at. A report naming two or three specific games points them straight at the
evidence.

**Say what looked wrong.** Perfect moves in a sharp position, long thinks on
obvious recaptures, a sudden change of strength mid-match — you noticed something,
and describing it is faster than making someone else find it.

**Write in English if you can.** Any moderator can then handle it, which usually
means sooner.

## What not to do

**Do not message moderators directly.** Direct messages are not tracked as
reports and will be slower than the form, not faster.

**Do not report in the forum**, and do not accuse anyone in game chat. Neither
starts an investigation; both can get you sanctioned instead.

**Do not send the same report repeatedly.** It is read once. Sending it again
does not move it up a queue.

## After you report

You will not hear back, and the account may keep playing for a while. Neither
means the report was ignored. Cheat investigation looks at a pattern across many
games, so acting immediately on one report would produce exactly the false
positives everyone wants avoided.

If the player is later marked, any rating you lost to them comes back
automatically.

## Losing to someone strong is not evidence

Being beaten badly, or by someone who improved fast, is not by itself a sign of
anything. Report what looks wrong, not what hurt.
