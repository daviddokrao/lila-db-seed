# Account deletion is pending

We have received your deletion request. Nothing more is needed from you.

## What happens

Your account is closed immediately, so nobody can sign in to it. The personal
data attached to it — your email address, your IP records, your preferences,
your private messages — is then erased.

## What does not get deleted

**Games you played.** A game belongs to two players. Deleting your side of it
would rewrite your opponent's history and their rating, so games stay, listed
against an anonymous account.

**Public posts made in shared spaces**, such as forum threads other people
replied to, may remain where removing them would break the conversation.

## Changed your mind

If the deletion has not finished, contact us quickly through the
[contact page](/contact) — once the data is gone we cannot bring it back.

You are welcome to register again afterwards. It will be a new account: ratings,
puzzle history and studies do not carry over.
